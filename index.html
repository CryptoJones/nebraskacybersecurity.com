Nebraska Cybersecurity

One state, one mission: defending Nebraska's networks, systems and data — from Omaha to the panhandle. This page highlights the organisations doing that work, and how you can plug in.

“An attack on one is an attack on us all.” — Nebraska CISO Bryce Bailey, on the state's whole-of-state approach

🏛️ State government & defense

The statewide backbone. Nebraska is moving from agency-by-agency security to a whole-of-state model: one connected defense across state agencies, counties, municipalities, schools and public-safety organisations.

State of Nebraska — Office of the CIO (OCIO)

Who they are: The state's central technology and security authority. Deputy CIO & Chief Information Security Officer Bryce Bailey leads the mission of reducing cyber risk across Nebraska's public sector.

  • Joint Security Operations Center (JSOC) — the day-to-day SOC: triages phishing, business-email compromise and active incidents, backed by dedicated firewall and governance/risk/compliance (GRC) teams covering IRS Pub 1075, CJIS, CMS/SSA requirements and third-party risk.
  • Planned statewide ISAC — combining the Nebraska Information Analysis Center (NIAC) and JSOC to share threat intelligence (IOCs, watchlists, tailored bulletins) with cities, counties and schools statewide, modelled on the Indiana and Maryland ISACs.
  • Workforce pipeline — a cyber internship program with Nebraska colleges that has already converted interns into full-time staff as the team grew from 5 people toward ~20.
  • SLCGP grant stewardship — shifting FY2026 State & Local Cybersecurity Grant dollars toward multi-jurisdictional, scale-economy purchases.

Plan: Nebraska Statewide Cybersecurity Plan (2023) · GovTech profile of the JSOC · State & Local Cybersecurity Grant Program (Nebraska)

Cybersecurity Planning Committee & the Statewide Cybersecurity Plan

A cross-sector committee — Office of the CIO, League of Nebraska Municipalities, Association of County Officials, Nebraska Public Power District, Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council, University of Nebraska, public-health and emergency-management representatives, and the Nebraska National Guard — owns the Nebraska Statewide Cybersecurity Plan. Vision: an ongoing, mature cybersecurity program that continually reduces cyber risk exposure of entities throughout Nebraska.

Nebraska National Guard — Joint Force Cyberspace

Provides cyber defense teams and the Cyber Protection Team that can be deployed to local jurisdictions in an emergency (as during the 2026 Lincoln County / North Platte incident). The Guard is a founding partner of Cyber Tatanka and supports the Army's Innovative Readiness Training program, training alongside Guard units from across the country and international partners (Czech Republic, Chile, Tanzania via the State Partnership Program).

Cyber Tatanka coverage · Governor's proclamation (2026)

Nebraska Information Analysis Center (NIAC)

Nebraska's federally recognised fusion center (Nebraska State Patrol). Under the statewide ISAC plan, NIAC's distribution channels become the conduit for pushing cyber threat intelligence out to every public-sector entity in the state.

NIAC · Investigative Services / Technical Crimes Unit — Cyber (state infrastructure, CIRT participation, training)

Nebraska Information Technology Commission (NITC)

The statewide technology commission that sets policy and informs the Governor and Legislature — including its annual Nebraska Cyber Security Conference (with Southeast Community College), a long-running gathering for Nebraska's public-sector cyber workforce.

🤝 Professional community & training

Cyber Strong Nebraska & Cyber Tatanka

Cyber Strong Nebraska is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 99-5121710) dedicated to enhancing cybersecurity awareness, education and resources across the state. Its flagship is Cyber Tatanka — Nebraska's premier defensive cyber range exercise, held annually at UNL's Kiewit Hall: two weeks of classroom training plus a live-fire range, run on isolated cloud ranges (Cloud Cyber Range) against escalating MITRE ATT&CK–mapped scenarios. By 2026 it had trained 750 professionals from 12+ critical-infrastructure sectors, five states/territories and six countries.

Cyber Strong Nebraska · cybertatanka.org · UNL College of Engineering

NEbraskaCERT — Cyber Security Forum (CSF)

The community's longest-running forum, built on two pillars: sharing of knowledge and applied research. CSF meets every third Wednesday of the month — with lunch, breakfast and online sessions — and hosts the annual joint security meeting (15th annual: November 18, 2026 at Johnny's Cafe in Omaha) that brings together security groups from across the metro.

nebraskacert.org · CSF · csf info

DC402 — Omaha DEF CON Group

Omaha's DEF CON group: a free, informal monthly meetup (second Tuesday, DJ's Dugout, Miracle Hills) with a talk followed by open discussion. Members span U.S. government, CTF players, digital forensics and law enforcement, with an active Slack channel keeping the community connected. DC402 is also the root of Kernelcon, Omaha's community hacker conference (Hilton Omaha, Mar 4–5, 2027).

dc402.org · kernelcon.org

Professional chapters

  • ISC2 Omaha–Lincoln Chapter — monthly chapter meetings (Omaha + online), certification study, and the CMMC and governance topics Nebraska's defense and public-sector workforce needs. isc2omaha.org
  • ISACA Omaha Chapter — monthly membership lunches at the Scott Conference Center (online option), an annual general meeting and a spring seminar; the IS audit, control and security profession's local home. engage.isaca.org/omahachapter
  • InfraGard — Omaha / Nebraska (FBI) — vetted private-sector partners sharing threat information with the FBI and DHS on cyber crime and critical-infrastructure protection; one of the first InfraGard chapters in the country (est. 1999). infragardnational.org
  • OWASP Eastern Nebraska — application-security community, joining the November 18, 2026 joint panel at Johnny's Cafe.
  • Cyber Security Omaha — a community hub that aggregates Nebraska's meetups, associations and conferences. cybersecurityomaha.com/community

Industry & critical-infrastructure partners

Nebraska's "who protects what" is a who's-who of the state's backbone: Nebraska Public Power District (NPPD), Lincoln Electric System (LES), Metropolitan Utilities District (MUD), Union Bank & Trust, Bryan Health, Union Pacific Railroad, the Nebraska Emergency Management Agency, and the University of Nebraska system — all standing partners of Cyber Tatanka and the statewide plan.

🎓 Education & workforce

University of Nebraska at Omaha (UNO)

Home of the Nebraska University Center for Cybersecurity (NebraskaCYBER) (est. 2001). UNO is an NSA National Center of Academic Excellence in Cybersecurity with both CAE-CD (Cyber Defense) and CAE-CO (Cyber Operations) designations, offering BS and MS degrees in Cybersecurity (campus + online). Students compete through Nullify, UNO's cybersecurity student organisation (est. 2011) — which recently ran a 30-hour, 30-challenge "30/30 CTF" for the College of IS&T's 30th anniversary.

NebraskaCYBER · BS in Cybersecurity · Nullify

University of Nebraska–Lincoln (UNL)

Hosts Cyber Tatanka at Kiewit Hall (College of Engineering) and offers a BS in Computer Science with a cybersecurity focus.

Bellevue University

Omaha-metro university with CAE-CD designation and both bachelor's and master's cybersecurity programs — a proven path for working professionals. Bellevue University also hosts NEbraskaCERT's evening CSF sessions.

Community & technical colleges

Metropolitan Community College (Omaha) — AAS in Cybersecurity, CAE-CD. Northeast Community College (Norfolk) — Information Security Certificate, CAE-CD. Southeast Community College (Lincoln) — co-host of the annual Nebraska Cyber Security Conference.

Offutt Air Force Base (USSTRATCOM)

Bellevue's Offutt AFB is home to U.S. Strategic Command and the 960th Cyberspace Operations Group / 960th Network Warfare Flight — a major cyber employer and mission partner in the Omaha metro.

🚀 Get involved

  1. Attend one meeting. DC402 (second Tuesday) or a NEbraskaCERT CSF (third Wednesday) — both free and welcoming to newcomers.
  2. Train on the range. Register for the next Cyber Tatanka — open to military, civilian and student defenders.
  3. Join a chapter. ISC2 Omaha–Lincoln for certifications, ISACA Omaha for audit/GRC, InfraGard for vetted FBI information sharing.
  4. Study or teach. UNO's NebraskaCYBER, Bellevue University, and MCC/NECC pipelines feed the statewide workforce — and the OCIO internship program hires from them.
  5. Share your story. This site is a living directory: if you're a Nebraska cyber organisation missing from this page, reach out and we'll add you.
